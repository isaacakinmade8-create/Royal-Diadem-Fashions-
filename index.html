Bespoke Styles

Made for you, and only you.

Bespoke means your outfit is designed and tailored from scratch to your measurements, taste and occasion. Nothing off the rack: you choose the style, fabric, colour and finishing, and we bring it to life with Royal Diadem craftsmanship.

  1. Consultation — tell us about your event and the look you have in mind.
  2. Design & fabric — we agree the style, fabric and details.
  3. Measurements & fittings — a precise fit, adjusted until it is perfect.
  4. Delivery — your finished piece, ready for your moment.
